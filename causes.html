What We Do

Our Focus Areas Across Southern Africa

👶 Orphan Care & Child Protection

Providing safe environments, care, and protection for orphaned and vulnerable children across Southern Africa.

📚 Education Support & Scholarships

Enabling access to quality education through scholarships and learning resources in rural communities.

🍲 Feeding & Nutrition Programmes

Ensuring food security and proper nutrition for children and families in underserved areas.

🏥 Healthcare & Medical Assistance

Providing essential healthcare services and medical support to underserved communities.

💙 Mental Health & Psychosocial Support

Offering mental wellness programs and psychosocial support for emotional well-being.

💼 Youth Skills Development & Employment

Empowering youth with skills training and pathways to sustainable employment.

🌱 Community Empowerment

Building resilient communities through sustainable development initiatives.

🚨 Disaster Relief & Humanitarian Response

Providing emergency aid and humanitarian support during crises and disasters.

🤖 AI, Robotics & IoT Education

Providing access to Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and IoT education for children in rural areas across Southern Africa. Aligned with national strategies including Zimbabwe's National AI Strategy and South Africa's 2027 rollout.

🏫 School Infrastructure Development

Supporting the $420 million drive to build and renovate schools in rural Zimbabwe and South Africa. We identify infrastructure needs and seek funding for school construction projects.
